About

Built
from the
North

STRATA are an emerging indie rock band from Preston, Lancashire — forging a sound that sits somewhere between raw guitar energy and atmospheric, layered songwriting.

Drawing on the grit of Northern England and the expansive indie tradition that surrounds them, STRATA are building something distinctly their own — honest, loud, and uncompromising.

Currently recording new material and playing shows across the North, this is a band at the beginning of something real.
